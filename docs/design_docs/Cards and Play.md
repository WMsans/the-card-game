# Cards & Play

## Bones

* Cards cost Tickets to be played  
* Cards take up board space  
* Cards have effects that augment the game state and may deal damage to bring about the end of the game

## Discussion

* What are these Tickets?  
  * Resource Deck  
    * Different Types?  
  * Generation per Turn  
    * Ramping Production?  
  * Tapping / Refreshing  
* The Board  
  * Maximum Board Size?  
    * How many cards / do they take up the same space?  
  * How do you use cards or their effects?  
* Combat  
  * How do cards “attack” / win the game?

## Decisions

* Resources → “Raffle Tickets”  
  * Reshuffle on deck empty  
    * Burn on reshuffling? → limited number of reshuffles until game end  
    * Discard deck that gets shuffled in on empty main deck  
  * Separate deck of energy  
  * Using the resource “taps” it and is refreshed at the start of your turn  
  * Each player gets \+2 per turn  
  * Any unused resources remain active and may be used by specific cards  
* The Board  
  * NO BOARD SIZE  
  * Tap a card to attack/use it  
* Combat  
  * \# of Reshuffles to Win  
  * Units played on a turn start tapped  
  * Units commit to an attack anything (unless otherwise stated)  
  * Defender unit hits back  
  * Units die if incoming damage is greater than health  
  * Units full heal after combat  
* Card Stat Power Budget (Median, No-Ability)  
  * 1 Cost \- 3 (= 1/2, 2/1, 0/3 etc.)  
  * 2 Cost \- 5  
  * 3 Cost \- 7  
  * 4 Cost \- 9  
  * 5 Cost \- 11  
  * 6 Cost \- 13  
  * 7 Cost \- 15  
  * 8 Cost \- 17  
  * 9 Cost \- 19  
  * 10 Cost \- 21  
    * Effects can nerf or buff this statline
