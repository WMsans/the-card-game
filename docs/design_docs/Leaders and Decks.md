## Bones

* Health-based Leaders / “Face”

* Leaders are the literal and figurative “face” of the deck and should generally be the most interesting and game-defining cards/effects

## Decisions

* Spells EXIST  
* Leaders  
  * Playable Cards  
  * Cost Cards to Play or can cost Resources  
  * Starts in hand regardless of mulligan  
  * If the Leader dies, it gets put into the Discard pile  
* Mono-color  
* Max Hand Size \= 5  
  * First Turn \- 5 discard 2 → 3 card starting hand \+ Leader  
  * Draw 1 per turn (including first turn)  
  * Discard cards over limit at end of turn  
* Deck Size \= 20  
  * 2 Dupes per Deck  
  * Leader is a separate card (not relevant to deck size)
