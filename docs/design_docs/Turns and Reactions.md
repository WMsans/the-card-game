# Turns & Reactions

## Bones

* There is a player who goes first  
* Each player acts sequentially after the other  
* Actions are done within your turn

## Discussion

* First Player  
  * The player going first usually has an innate advantage \- how will this be addressed?  
    * OPTCG: 2 don per turn rule  
    * Hearthstone: The Coin  
    * MtG: “Fuck you”  
    * Pokemon: Can’t attack first turn  
    * All: Don’t get to draw a card  
* Reactions  
  * Should there be any response from the other player while you are doing an action on your turn?  
    * Counterspells  
    * Traps  
    * Initiated Combats and Decisions

## Decisions

* 1st Player  
  * Starts with 1 Resource  
  * 2nd Player starts with 2 Resources  
* Reactions  
  * **Traps**  
    * **Purpose** \- prevent OTK  
    * Play independently (not attached unless a Trap is otherwise stated)  
    * Keep low-impact and uncommon  
    * By default secret, but can be modified by the card’s effect if applicable
